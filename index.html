1. Estimate occupancy

The image detector counts visible people. Motion and the timetable provide additional clues.

2. Check for disagreement

If a class is scheduled or motion is detected while the image looks empty, Phantom asks for a check.

3. Look for possible waste

Low occupancy confidence plus equipment left on and unusually high power can raise a room's waste score.

4. Recommend, never force

A person must verify the room. The prototype does not switch off lights, AC, or projectors.

Method notes & limitations

  • The energy forecast uses the positive difference between live load and an expected idle baseline, multiplied by a demo time window. Actual savings require meter validation.
  • Occupancy estimates can miss people, quiet study, accessibility needs, or camera blind spots. A low count alone is never proof that a room is empty.
  • Room-level repeated signals are illustrative seed data until real time-stamped records are connected.
  • For a pilot, calibrate per-room baselines, set a minimum verification dwell time, and involve facilities staff and room users.
Governance & trust

People come before kilowatts.

Energy suggestions should be explainable, reversible, and safe — even when the model feels confident.

ACTIVE · NON-NEGOTIABLE

The Kindness Rule

A recommendation is not a command. Phantom prioritizes room-user comfort, accessibility, safety, and faculty intent above estimated energy savings. The system cannot shut equipment off automatically.

Human override

Anyone authorized can mark a room as “Keep equipment on,” add a reason, and choose a review duration. That room is removed from active shutdown-style recommendations for the override window.

Conflict means verify

When camera, motion, and timetable disagree, the system requests a human check. It does not guess that the room is empty and it does not issue a control action.

Explain every flag

Each warning lists the evidence: camera count, motion, schedule, live load versus baseline, and the logic behind its score.

Privacy-aware by design

Use anonymous occupancy counts, process at the edge where possible, apply retention limits, and avoid face recognition or identity tracking.

Safeguards in this prototype

  • Automatic power control is disabled.
  • Override requires a reason and duration.
  • Overrides are visible in the room state and activity log.
  • Any conflict or stale signal triggers verification.
  • Scores are labelled estimates, not verified savings.
